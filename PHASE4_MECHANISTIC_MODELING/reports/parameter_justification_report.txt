GENE-TO-PARAMETER MAPPINGS:

SPP1:
  Parameter: k_immune
  Direction: High -> decrease
  Max adjustment: ±30%
  Rationale: SPP1 (osteopontin) suppresses immune surveillance via integrin signaling, promoting tumor immune evasion. High SPP1 associated with immunosuppressive TME in NSCLC. Ref: Rittling et al., Semin Cancer Biol 2020;76:120-131.
  Evidence: [LITERATURE]

COL1A1:
  Parameter: V_max
  Direction: High -> increase
  Max adjustment: ±25%
  Rationale: COL1A1 (collagen type I) is a key ECM component. High expression indicates active ECM remodeling and desmoplasia, enabling tumor expansion and increasing effective carrying capacity. Ref: Chen et al., Cancer Cell Int 2022;22:246.
  Evidence: [LITERATURE]

MMP1:
  Parameter: V_max
  Direction: High -> increase
  Max adjustment: ±20%
  Rationale: MMP1 (matrix metalloproteinase-1) degrades ECM, facilitating invasion and expansion. High MMP1 correlates with advanced stage in NSCLC. Ref: Hojilla CV, et al. Front Oncol 2022;12:877643.
  Evidence: [LITERATURE]

CXCL8:
  Parameter: rho
  Direction: High -> decrease
  Max adjustment: ±25%
  Rationale: CXCL8 (IL-8) recruits neutrophils (MDSCs) rather than effective CTLs. High CXCL8 in NSCLC promotes immunosuppressive TME and angiogenesis, reducing effective anti-tumor immune response. Ref: Schalper KA, et al. Clin Cancer Res 2017;23(16):4771-4782.
  Evidence: [LITERATURE]

EGLN3:
  Parameter: alpha
  Direction: High -> increase
  Max adjustment: ±20%
  Rationale: EGLN3 (PHD3) is a hypoxia sensor. High EGLN3 indicates hypoxic adaptation, which in NSCLC is associated with aggressive phenotype and HIF-1alpha driven proliferation. Ref: Henze AT, et al. Cancer Res 2010;70(13):5351-5361.
  Evidence: [LITERATURE]
