4. RNA-seq vs MICROARRAY PLATFORM CONSIDERATIONS

   TCGA: Illumina RNA-seq, STAR counts → CPM normalization → log2(CPM+1)
   GEO:  Affymetrix microarray, log2-transformed intensities (GPL570/GPL15048)

   Key differences:
   - RNA-seq counts are discrete and library-size dependent; microarray
     intensities are continuous and probe-affinity dependent
   - log2(CPM+1) and log2(intensity) are not on the same absolute scale,
     but both are log-transformed and approximately comparable for
     relative expression patterns
   - Standardization (z-score using TCGA training mean/std) partially
     mitigates scale differences but does not eliminate probe-specific
     hybridization biases
   - No batch correction was performed (per protocol)
